A hand-crafted player built from scratch for both desktop and mobile — with features that rival native apps.

10-Band Equalizer
Full 31 Hz – 16 kHz graphic EQ with a Preamp/Boost slider, built-in presets, and a one-click reset. Available in the standalone player; your curve is synced across sessions.

Swipe to control brightness and volume (fullscreen in Overlay mode). Double-tap left / right to seek ±10s. Long-press anywhere for instant 2× speed with ripple feedback. Horizontal swipe seeks by distance (5–60s).

play_circle How It Works

Two ways to play.

WebPlayer works on any page with a video element, or automatically intercepts raw stream URLs from network traffic.

01
layers
Overlay Mode
Injects a custom gesture layer and controls directly on top of any video — without replacing it.
1 Navigate to any page with a <video> element.
2 On desktop, hover the video; on mobile, tap the video area to reveal ▶ Launch WebPlayer.
3 Click it — the full control panel appears instantly.
02
open_in_new
Standalone Mode
For raw stream URLs, WebPlayer intercepts the request and offers a dedicated full-window player tab.
1 Open or navigate to any .m3u8 or .mpd stream URL.
2 A notification appears offering to launch the player.
3 Click Launch Player for the full experience with the 10-band EQ.
