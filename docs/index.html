WebPlayer

Your videos, reimagined.

A custom, ad-free player with gestures, EQ, SponsorBlock, and HLS/DASH — for Chrome and Firefox.

Install extension View on GitHub

Capabilities

Built for people who watch a lot.

Overlay any page video, or open raw streams in a dedicated player with the full toolkit.

10-band EQ

Graphic equalizer from 31 Hz–16 kHz with preamp, presets, and sync across sessions in the standalone player.

Smart gestures

Brightness, volume, distance-based seek, double-tap ±10s, and long-press 2× — tuned for desktop and mobile.

SponsorBlock

Skip sponsors, intros, outros, and self-promo automatically, with category badges and one-tap undo.

HLS & DASH

Native adaptive streams with quality, subtitles, audio tracks, and Manifest V3 CORS handling.

Video enhancer

Sharpen, saturation, contrast, and brightness — plus Anime, Cinema, and Sports presets.

Material themes

Blue, Amethyst, and Emerald themes with glassmorphic controls that follow your accent.

How it works

Two ways to play.

Use the overlay on any site with a video, or launch the full standalone tab for streams.

Overlay

Injects gestures and controls over the existing video without replacing the page.

  1. Open any page with a <video> element.
  2. Reveal Launch WebPlayer on the video.
  3. Play with custom controls, enhancer, and gestures.

Standalone

Intercepts .m3u8 / .mpd traffic and opens a dedicated full-window player.

  1. Navigate to a page that loads a stream URL.
  2. Accept the stream detected prompt.
  3. Get EQ, themes, CC, quality, and persistence.

Support

Manifest V3. Major browsers.

Load the Chrome or Firefox build from the repo — Edge and Brave work with the Chromium package.
